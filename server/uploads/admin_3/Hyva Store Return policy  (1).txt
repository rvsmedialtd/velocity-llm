Returns Policy
We want you to love every purchase with us. If something isn’t right, our policy makes returns and exchanges simple and fair.
Return Window
* Items are eligible for return within 30 days of delivery (online orders) or purchase (in-store).
Eligibility Criteria
* Items must be unworn, unused, unwashed, and in their original condition and packaging.
* All tags and labels must remain attached.
* Items will be inspected upon return. We reserve the right to refuse a refund for items that are worn, damaged, soiled, or in an unsuitable condition.
Exceptions (Non-Returnable Items)
* Underwear and Swimwear: Non-returnable due to hygiene reasons.
* Socks: Can only be returned if still sealed in original packaging.
* Bottles/accessories: Non-returnable for hygiene reasons.
* Personalised/Custom products: Non-returnable.
* Final Sale items: (if you use these) Not eligible for return, refund, or exchange. These will be clearly marked at checkout.
* Items from Pop-up Stores or Special Events: Not eligible for return.
Returns Process
1. Register your return through our online Returns Portal with your order details and reason for return. Here is the portal URL https://rma.rvsmedia.com/
2. You can also raise a return by logging in to your account and click on the My Orders you will see list of all the orders. Click on the return button next to the order ID and follow the instructions on the portal.
3. Pack each return from a different order separately (do not combine returns).
4. Include proof of purchase (original receipt or e-receipt) for in-store purchases.
5. Retain your return postage receipt until your return is confirmed.
Exchanges
* Exchanges are allowed for size of the same item only (subject to availability).
* We cannot pre-reserve exchange items. Exchanges will be processed only after your returned item is scanned/received at our warehouse.
* If the requested exchange is out of stock, you will receive a refund.
Refund Information
* Refunds are made to the original payment method.
* If you used a gift card, refunds will first be applied to the gift card.
* Refunds typically take up to 7 days to process after the returned item is received at our warehouse, with an additional 5 days for the money to reach your account.
Shipping
* Unless stated otherwise, return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer.
* Please retain your shipping/postage receipt in case proof of return is required.
Faulty or Incorrect Items
If you receive a faulty or incorrect item, please contact our Customer Support for a resolution before returning.
Contact
If you have questions about your return or our policy, reach out to our Customer Support team via our website.
